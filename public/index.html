Players with the same score share a place and all get that place's points. Players tied for last all lose the last-place points.

Team vs team: every player on the winning team gets +1, every player on the losing team −1. A draw scores 0 for all.

Quiz: players collect quiz points during the game. When it ends, they're ranked by quiz points and scored like a normal game.

Winner-only games: the winner gets the 1st-place points above, everyone else gets 0.
